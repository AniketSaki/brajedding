Every week, without fail, Brajesh asks for Friday nominations. Every week, without fail, everyone is tired of it. "It's Friday, who nominate" has outlived several films it was nominating.

He is the club's loudest heckler for due process. CEC-ji must take strict action. CEOji must resign. Mubians have been disenfranchised. Ask him to take a position of responsibility himself, though, and he is suddenly very busy.

He is always late to meetings. He does not understand youth slang, and explaining it to him only makes it worse.

## The attendance record

The club keeps an attendance sheet, and the sheet does not lie.

| Year | Sessions attended | Club ranking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 | 27 | 5th |
| 2024 | 42 | 3rd |
| 2025 | 28 | 4th |
| 2026 (so far) | 20 | joint 3rd |

Then there is 2025. He was there for all of January, February and May, and then he started to fade. One session in July. **Zero in August, September and October.** Fourteen meetings in a row without Brajesh, from *Ash Is Purest White* on 5 August to *Four Nights of a Dreamer* on 4 November. Investigations are ongoing.

## The critic

His favourite movie is *Ramen Shop*. This tells you most of what you need to know.

He is also the club's softest critic. Since 2025, about 4 in 5 of his ratings have been a thumbs-up, against roughly 2 in 3 for everyone else. Brajesh does not dislike films. Films simply occasionally fail to align with him.
