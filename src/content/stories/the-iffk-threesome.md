Three men set out for the International Film Festival of Kerala: the CEO, Brajesh and Raouf. Vishnu made a guest appearance, as Vishnu does.

Brajesh arrived with one goal and he was fully aligned on it: catch as many screenings as humanly possible. Queues, back-to-back slots, films nobody else had heard of. He was there for all of it.

Raouf was not. Midway through, Raouf abandoned the team to go and write a quiz in some distant city. No due process. No Friday nomination. Just gone.

## The sleeping arrangements

For three nights, Brajesh and Raouf shared a bed. The festival catalogue does not say what was screened in that room, and the two of them have never released a director's cut.

Saranya, consider this your formal disclosure.
